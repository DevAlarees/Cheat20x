FAQ

What is Cheat20x?

A menu interface with Combat, Visuals, Misc and Settings tabs.

Do the controls on this site do anything?

They run in a demo of the menu. They change what you see here, such as values, colors and saved settings, and nothing else.

Can I save my settings?

Yes. Open Settings in the menu, name your config and press Save. Load restores it and Reset returns every option to default.
